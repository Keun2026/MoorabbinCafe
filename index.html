About Our Cafe

Nestled in the heart of Moorabbin, Moorabbin Cafe offers a warm, relaxing atmosphere designed for local residents, students, teachers, and workers. Whether you are grabbing your morning espresso, settling in for a study session, or catching up with friends, we pride ourselves on exceptional service and quality locally roasted coffee beans.

Our Story & Heritage

Founded in 2015, Moorabbin Cafe began as a small neighborhood dream to bring specialty third-wave coffee culture to South Road. Originally an old industrial workshop storage space, we meticulously transformed the venue into a cozy sanctuary that honors Moorabbin’s rich mid-century heritage. Over the years, we’ve grown from a quick morning pitstop into a vibrant community hub where every cup is crafted with passion, community spirit, and a commitment to sustainable local sourcing.
